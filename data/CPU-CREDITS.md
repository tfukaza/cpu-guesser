# CPU reference collection

Generated by `node scripts/build-cpu-library.mjs`. Primary image license and creator metadata were retrieved from Wikimedia Commons and original photographers’ Flickr pages; retrieval dates and evidence are preserved in `data/research/`. Model/family labels, dates and provisional dimensions were discovered through [The Data Drop / Sheets Works](https://sheets.works/data-viz/cpu-dies). Dates and dimensions from the discovery catalog await independent verification. Supplemental records retain field-specific primary evidence and measurement/estimation notes.

This credits the original creators; Wikimedia Commons is the repository. No reference-only photo is redistributed. Supplemental primary records and rejected candidates are preserved alongside the original Commons evidence. Licensed derivatives must carry the creator, source, license and modification notice. `data/research/commons-batches.json` preserves the original metadata, including attribution strings and source URLs. Excluded non-CPU dies and annotated artwork remain in the research evidence; unresolved sources are in `data/research/pending-cpus.json`.
